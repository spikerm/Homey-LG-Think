Bring compatible LG ThinQ appliances into Homey for convenient control, status information and smart automation. The first release focuses on LG ThinQ washers and washer-dryers, including program control and a Smart Washing widget that can schedule a wash within a deadline using Homey Energy dynamic electricity prices.

The app is community-developed and is not an official LG Electronics app. Supported functions depend on the appliance model and the capabilities LG makes available through ThinQ.
